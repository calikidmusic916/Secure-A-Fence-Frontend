📜 Rental Agreement & Terms

Secure-A-Fence Inc. Equipment Rental Agreement

1. RENTAL PERIOD & BILLING: The rental period commences on the day the equipment is delivered to the jobsite and continues on a month-to-month basis until the Lessee formally requests a pickup. Billing will be automatically processed every 30 days based on the recurring monthly rate.

2. DELIVERY & PICKUP: Lessee must ensure clear access to the jobsite for delivery and pickup. Standard delivery fees apply. A pickup request must be submitted via the customer portal at least 48 hours in advance.

3. DAMAGE & LOSS FEES: Lessee assumes full responsibility for the equipment while on their jobsite. Equipment must be returned in the same condition as received, normal wear and tear excepted. The following replacement fees will be charged for damaged, destroyed, or stolen equipment:
• Fence Panel (Any Size): $150.00 per panel
• Base Stand: $35.00 per stand
• Connector Clip / Hardware: $10.00 per unit
• Custom Gates: Up to $300.00 depending on style

4. ALTERATIONS: Lessee shall not alter, cut, weld, or permanently attach signs to the fence panels. Temporary banners must be removed prior to pickup.

5. INDEMNIFICATION: Secure-A-Fence Inc. is not liable for any injuries, property damage, or security breaches resulting from the use, placement, or failure of the temporary fencing. The Lessee agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Secure-A-Fence Inc. against any such claims.
